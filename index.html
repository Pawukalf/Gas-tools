Quick guide
  1. Top-up: enter the current mix and pressure, then the target mix and final pressure.
  2. Empty: enter the target mix and final pressure.
  3. Select Fill for gauge targets or Add for individual gas amounts.
  4. For trimix, select O₂ first or He first. O₂ first is the default.
  5. Follow each step in order. A grey SKIP means no gas is required for that step.
